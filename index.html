00:06Questions

Everything you're probably wondering.

What's your turnaround?

Short-form edits usually land within 24 hours. Long-form and VSLs take 48–72 hours depending on length and how much motion graphics is involved. Rush work is possible — tell me the deadline upfront.

How many revisions?

Two rounds included on every project. In practice I'd rather get it right than get it done, so I don't nickel-and-dime small tweaks.

Do you write the script, or just edit?

I edit — but I structure heavily at the cut, reordering and rebuilding hooks so the video holds attention. Have a script? I'll cut to it. Have raw talking-head footage and no structure? I'll build the structure.

What do you need to start?

Raw footage in a shared folder, your brand assets and fonts if you have them, and one or two reference videos showing the style you want. That's enough for a first cut.

Can you match our existing brand?

Yes. Holding one consistent look, palette and typography across every deliverable is a core part of how I work with agencies.

Do you work on retainer?

Yes, and most of my work is exactly that — ongoing monthly volume for agencies and creators. Per-project is fine too.

Which ratios do you deliver?

9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, 1:1 and 4:5 for in-feed, 16:9 for YouTube, VSLs and podcasts — every ratio you need, from the same edit.

How do we start?

Message me on WhatsApp with what you're working on. I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right editor for it — and what I'd do differently.

Let's make your next video the one that performs.

Tell me about the offer and the footage. If I'm not the right editor for it, I'll say so — and point you at what would work better.
